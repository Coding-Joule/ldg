Beijing Lyric Design Group Co., Ltd. (LDG), founded in 1993, is a nationwide integrated design and consulting practice holding Grade A qualifications in architectural design and urban-rural planning. LDG serves as a council member of the Beijing Engineering Survey and Design Association and a member of the China Association of City Planning, China National Association of Engineering Consultants, Architectural Society of China, Beijing Natural Resources Assets Association, Haidian District Federation of Industry and Commerce, and Balizhuang Subdistrict Chamber of Commerce. With a strong professional network and industry resources, the practice actively participates in technical development, standards, and academic exchange. From its Beijing headquarters, LDG works with five regional branches and 25 secondary-city service offices. Guided by the principle “to build a profession, first build the people,” the group has cultivated technical experts, designers, and management professionals and provides high-level engineering design and integrated consulting services for clients and urban-rural development across China. Its work spans urban planning, architecture, landscape design, and project management, with continued innovation in design thinking and technical methods. Projects have been delivered in more than 20 provinces, municipalities and autonomous regions in China as well as internationally, receiving numerous awards.

Since 1993, LDG has completed more than 300 design projects worldwide, with a cumulative designed gross floor area exceeding 100 million square metres. Its work extends across more than 20 provinces, municipalities and autonomous regions in China and to international cities including London, Dubai and Los Angeles. Major projects include the interior renovation of the National Centre for the Performing Arts (Special Contribution Award), the southern viewing platform of Beijing Central Axis, Zhuhai Jiuzhouwan Blue Sea Tower, a flagship experience centre for Chinese new-energy vehicles, Miyun Shihao International Leisure Sports Centre (winning scheme in collaboration with Germany’s G&P), Phase II of Beijing Lido Hotel, Toyota Building, Xinjiang Hotan Anju Housing Project (Contribution Award for Xinjiang Aid), and Beijing Red Star Macalline Global Home Living Plaza.
